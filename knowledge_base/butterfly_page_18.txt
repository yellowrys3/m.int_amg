#  Smile Enhancement
##  Smile Enhancement
We are proud to serve our community and provide the people of San Jose with dental care that meets all their smile needs. From routine appointments to complex restorations, our team can take care of most of your dental needs. Come to us with your concerns, questions, or goals for your smile and let us help you tackle them head-on to achieve a healthy, fully functioning smile.

##  Dental Bonding
Bonding is a common cosmetic procedure that can be done comfortably in our office. Unlike other treatments, bonding typically doesn't require any type of anesthetic to complete.
##  Smile Makeover
A smile makeover in dentistry refers to a comprehensive approach to improving the appearance of a person's smile through various cosmetic and restorative dental procedures. It is customized to meet the individual's aesthetic goals and dental health needs, aiming for an enhanced, balanced, and natural-looking smile.
![Smile Makeover Butterfly Dental Care San Jose CA](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201440%201079'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

##  Teeth Whitening
A single visit of teeth whitening treatment that can bring you a bright smile and build up your confidence. Our teeth whitening systems provide quick and effective results which become the most popular smile makeover procedure in our office.
##  Porcelain Veneers
A thin, custom-made shell of porcelain material designed to cover the front teeth imperfection such as discoloration, misalignment, gaps, or chipped tooth structure. This procedure will enhance your look and smile to the next level.
![Porcelain Veneers Butterfly-Dental-Care-San-Jose-CA](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201440%20960'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
##  Liplase®
Liplase® helps to plump lips by stimulating collagen and elastin production. This non-surgical procedure can create volume, and give you natural looking lips that are full and youthful. Liplase® is perfect for patients that have thin lips, or for those that have lost volume due to aging.
##  Fotona 3D®/ Smoothlase®
SmoothLase® is a non-invasive, laser-based facial rejuvenation treatment designed to improve skin tightness, reduce wrinkles, and promote collagen production. Fotona 3D® refers to the multi-step process that treats skin from both the inside and outside. This innovative treatment offering natural-looking results with minimal discomfort and downtime​.

##  Invisalign®
Invisalign® is a discrete treatment that uses clear aligner trays to gently move teeth into place. Invisalign is a popular alternative to traditional braces, giving patients beautiful straight smiles.

##  Gummy Smile
A gummy smile, also known as excessive gingival display, occurs when a person smiles and a disproportionate amount of gum tissue is visible above the upper teeth. To bring natural looking smile back, we offer non-invasive Botox(R) treatment to surgical treatment.
![Gummy Smile Treatment Butterfly-Dental-Care-San-Jose-CA](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201440%20960'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
![Gum Pigmentation-Butterfly-Dental-Care-San-Jose-CA](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201440%20960'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
##  Gum Pigmentation
Gingival hyperpigmentation can affect confidence and your smile. With simple non invasive treatment, we can bring your bright smile back.
##  Snap On Smile®
Snap On Smile® is a removable dental appliance that fits over your existing teeth to instantly transform your smile. Made from high-tech dental resin, it's an affordable, non-invasive solution for stained, chipped, crooked, or missing teeth. This custom-made appliance requires no drilling, shots, or adhesives, making it ideal for patients seeking a temporary or reversible smile enhancement option.
