## 6 Key Elements of a Great Prompt
Master these essential components to craft prompts that produce stunning, consistent results.
01
### Establish the Shot
Use cinematography terms that match your intended genre. Include shot scale or category-specific characteristics.
Example
“Wide establishing shot, Close-up portrait, Aerial drone view”
02
### Set the Scene
Describe lighting conditions, color palette, surface textures, and atmosphere to establish mood.
Example
“Golden hour lighting with warm tones, foggy atmosphere with blue undertones”
03
### Describe the Action
Write the core action as a natural sequence, flowing clearly from beginning to end.
Example
“Character walks slowly toward the camera, then turns to look at the horizon”
04
### Define the Character
Include age, hairstyle, clothing, and distinguishing features. Express emotion through physical cues.
Example
“Young woman with flowing red hair, wearing a vintage leather jacket, determined expression”
05
### Camera Movement
Specify how and when the camera moves. Describe how subjects appear after the movement.
Example
“Camera slowly pans left, then zooms in on the character's face”
06
### Describe the Audio
Clearly describe ambient sound, music, speech, or singing. Use quotes for spoken dialogue.
Example
“Sound of waves crashing, distant seagulls, soft piano music in the background”
💡
#### Pro Tip: The Flow Method
Write your prompt as a single flowing paragraph rather than bullet points. Use present tense verbs and describe the scene as if it's happening right now. Aim for 4-8 descriptive sentences that naturally flow from establishing the shot to describing the audio.
## Tips for Best Results
Follow these guidelines to consistently generate high-quality videos that match your vision.
### Write as a Single Paragraph
Craft your prompt as one flowing paragraph instead of bullet points. This creates better narrative coherence.
### Use Present Tense
Always use present tense verbs for action and movement. This creates immediacy and clarity.
### Match Detail to Shot Scale
Close-ups need more detail than wide shots. Focus your descriptive energy where it matters most.
### Iterate Freely
LTX is designed for fast experimentation. Don't aim for perfection on the first try.
  * Write as a flowing narrative paragraph
  * Use present tense for all actions
  * Describe camera movement relative to subjects
  * Include specific lighting and atmosphere details
  * Use quotation marks for spoken dialogue
  * Keep prompts to 4-8 sentences


  * Use bullet points or fragmented descriptions
  * Write in past or future tense
  * Describe camera movement in absolute terms
  * Forget to mention lighting conditions
  * Include dialogue without quotation marks
  * Write overly long or complex prompts

## Ready-to-Use Templates
Use these structured templates as starting points for your prompts. Replace the bracketed sections with your own content.
### Cinematic Scene
Perfect for dramatic movie-style shots with professional cinematography.
Collapse
[SHOT TYPE] of [SUBJECT] in [LOCATION]. The lighting is [LIGHTING DESCRIPTION], creating [MOOD/ATMOSPHERE]. [CAMERA MOVEMENT]. [CHARACTER ACTION WITH EMOTION]. Sound: [AUDIO DESCRIPTION].
### Character Portrait
Focus on character details and emotional expression.
Expand
[SHOT SCALE] of [CHARACTER DESCRIPTION]. [LIGHTING DETAILS]. The character is [ACTION/POSE], with [EMOTIONAL EXPRESSION DETAILS]. [CLOTHING/ACCESSORIES]. Background: [SETTING DESCRIPTION]. Sound: [AUDIO].
### Nature Documentary
Capture the beauty of natural landscapes and wildlife.
Expand
[SHOT TYPE] of [LANDSCAPE/SCENE]. The time of day is [TIME], with [LIGHTING CONDITIONS]. [WEATHER/ATMOSPHERE DETAILS]. [CAMERA MOVEMENT]. [ELEMENTS IN MOTION]. Sound: [NATURAL AUDIO].
### Urban Scene
City life, architecture, and street photography style.
Expand
[SHOT TYPE] of [URBAN LOCATION]. The time is [TIME OF DAY], with [LIGHTING]. [ARCHITECTURE DETAILS]. [PEOPLE/ACTIVITY DESCRIPTION]. [CAMERA MOVEMENT]. Sound: [CITY AUDIO].
### Fantasy World
Magical scenes with otherworldly elements and atmosphere.
Expand
[SHOT TYPE] of [FANTASY SETTING]. The atmosphere is [MAGICAL ELEMENTS], with [LIGHTING EFFECTS]. [CREATURES/CHARACTERS]. [MAGICAL ACTION]. [CAMERA MOVEMENT]. Sound: [FANTASY AUDIO].
#### Template Legend
`[SHOT TYPE]`Wide shot, Close-up, Aerial, etc.
`[SUBJECT]`Main focus of the scene
`[LOCATION]`Where the scene takes place
`[LIGHTING]`Natural light, neon, golden hour
`[CAMERA MOVEMENT]`Pan, zoom, tracking shot
`[AUDIO]`Ambient sounds, music, dialogue
## Mistakes to Avoid
Learn from common errors that prevent prompts from producing great results.
Using Bullet Points
The Problem
Listing scene elements as bullet points creates disjointed, incoherent videos.
Write as a flowing narrative paragraph that connects all elements naturally.
❌ Don't
• Character walks • Camera pans left • Golden lighting • City background
✅ Do
A wide shot of a young professional walking confidently through a sunlit city street. Golden afternoon light filters through tall buildings, casting long shadows. The camera slowly pans left, following the character as they move through the bustling urban environment.
Writing in Past Tense
Vague Descriptions
Forgetting Lighting
Overly Long Prompts
