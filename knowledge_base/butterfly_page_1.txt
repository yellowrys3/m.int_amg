##  Orthodontic Treatment for Teens
As children grow into their teenage years, orthodontic concerns often become more apparent. Dr. Chen provides comprehensive orthodontic evaluations and treatment options for teens who need help achieving properly aligned teeth and a healthy bite.
Early orthodontic intervention can address issues such as:
  * Crowded or crooked teeth
  * Overbites, underbites, or crossbites
  * Gaps between teeth
  * Jaw alignment problems


We offer modern orthodontic solutions that fit into busy teen lifestyles, helping young patients achieve confident smiles they'll love for years to come.
##  Orthodontic Screenings
We recommend orthodontic screenings by age seven to identify potential alignment issues early. During these evaluations, Dr. Chen assesses your child's bite, jaw growth, and tooth positioning to determine if orthodontic treatment may be beneficial in the future.
Early detection allows us to:
  * Monitor development patterns
  * Plan treatment timing for optimal results
  * Address issues before they become more complex
  * Potentially reduce overall treatment time

##  Children's Dentistry FAQ
###  When should my child first visit the dentist? 
We recommend scheduling your child's first dental visit within six months of their first tooth appearing, or by their first birthday at the latest.
###  How often should children visit the dentist? 
Children should have dental checkups and cleanings every six months to maintain optimal oral health and catch any issues early.
###  What should I do if my child has a dental emergency? 
Contact our office immediately at (408) 709-7366. We provide emergency care and will guide you through the appropriate steps to take.
###  How can I help my child feel comfortable about dental visits? 
Read books about dental visits, practice good oral hygiene at home, and maintain a positive attitude about dental care. Our team is experienced in working with anxious children and will help make the experience pleasant.
