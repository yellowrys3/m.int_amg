Since then we’ve served the community as a family oriented dental health clinic that takes care of patients in all age groups, from kids to elders. We thrive to provide a comfortable environment and long lasting dental treatment.

###  ADVANCED TECHNOLOGY
In order to bring you the best care possible, we invest in leading-edge technology  
to make your appointments as efficient and effective as possible.
##  Vizilite Oral Cancer Screen
## Vizilite Oral Cancer Screen
Oral cancer screening technology utilizes specialized fluorescence visualization devices to detect potentially cancerous and pre-cancerous lesions that may not be visible to the naked eye.
## Intraoral 3D Scanning and Scanner Technology
## Intraoral 3D Scanning and Scanner Technology
Intraoral 3D scanning is a state-of-the-art digital technology that uses advanced scanners to capture precise three-dimensional images of teeth and gums.
## CBCT
## CBCT
CBCT is a specialized type of X-ray technology that provides three-dimensional (3D) images of a patient's teeth, bones, airway, TMJ and nerve pathways.
## Implant Navigation
## Implant Navigation
Navident is a dynamic navigation system for dental implant surgery that provides real-time guidance and tracking.
## Digital X Rays
## Digital X Rays
Dental digital x-rays are a modern imaging technique used in dentistry to capture images of teeth, gums, and jaw structures.
## IntraOral Cameras
## IntraOral Cameras
Intraoral cameras are allowing us to capture high-resolution images of the inside of a patient’s mouth.
##  Fotona LightWalker Dental Laser
##  Fotona LightWalker Dental Laser
Fotona laser technology represents a state-of-the-art approach to various dental procedures.
## Fotona StarFormer
## Fotona StarFormer
The StarFormer is a magnetic stimulation device that helps build and tone muscles to help shape and tone the body.
##  Emergency Dental Care
Dental emergencies aren’t something you expect, but when they happen, it’s important to get to your dentist’s office quickly. We set aside time in our schedule each day to see and treat emergencies so you don’t have to wait around in pain. Whether you have a sudden and severe toothache, lose a filling or crown, or knock out a tooth, call Butterfly Dental right away. We want to save your smile, and the quicker you come in, the more likely we can!
