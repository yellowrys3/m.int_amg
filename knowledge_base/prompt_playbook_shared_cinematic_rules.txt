# Shared Cinematic Rules for Veo + LTX (m.int Contract)

This playbook defines cross-model prompt defaults for short dental marketing videos.

## Prompt structure contract

Always provide:

1. Objective
2. Format/Duration
3. Scene Breakdown
4. Camera/Lighting/Motion
5. Audio/Mood
6. On-screen Text
7. Final CTA Frame
8. Negative Constraints

## Format defaults

- Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical.
- Runtime target: 8-12 seconds.
- Keep scene plan concise and production-ready.

## Scene and motion guidance

- Limit scene count to short, coherent beats.
- Use clear camera verbs: push-in, pan, static close-up, rack focus.
- Keep lighting simple, natural, and consistent with healthcare tone.

## Safety and brand constraints

- No diagnosis language.
- No guaranteed outcomes or cure language.
- No unsupported promotions or invented claims.
- No graphic dental imagery.
- CTA should invite consultation, booking, or direct question.
