##  Restorative Dentistry
Restorative dentistry involves a variety of treatments and procedures that improve both the function and aesthetics of your smile. Restorative dentistry can range from procedures that improve the way that you chew and eat to cosmetic options that enhance the look and feel of your teeth. At Butterfly Dental we offer a range of quality restorative procedures to fit your needs and will develop a personalized treatment plan to help you meet your smile goals. 
[ Learn more ](https://www.butterflydental.net/restorative-dentistry/)
##  Dental Implants
Dental implants are a popular choice for patients seeking a permanent restorative solution to damaged, broken, or missing teeth. Implants are durable, and feel like your natural teeth, giving patients a long lasting beautiful smile.

##  Laser Dentistry
ComfortLase™ is an advanced laser technology used in dentistry and medical primarily for pain management, soft tissue treatments, and promoting wound healing. It helps reduce inflammation, pain, and accelerates tissue regeneration, making it an effective option for healing after surgery, chemotherapy, TMJ, trauma.

##  Children’s Dentistry
The ADA recommends that the first dental visit take place within six months after the first tooth appears, but no later than a child's first birthday. It is important to bring your child to see the dentist once every six months. This visit allows us to examine their teeth and provide them with essential teeth cleaning and check their growth pattern.
[ Learn more ](https://www.butterflydental.net/childrens-dentistry/)
