# 🎬 AI Video Prompt Engineering Guide — 2026
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#-ai-video-prompt-engineering-guide--2026)
**Date:** March 20, 2026 
**Sources:** LTX Studio, TrueFan AI, Simplified, Envato Elements, Magic Prompt, Medium
> _The difference between a generic AI video and a cinematic one is entirely in the prompt._
* * *
## 🧠 How AI Video Models Read Prompts
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#-how-ai-video-models-read-prompts)
Modern video models (Sora 2, Runway Gen-4.5, Kling 2.6, Veo 3.1) interpret prompts differently from image generators:
 * **Token priority** — The first 20–30 words carry the most weight. Front-load the critical elements.
 * **Temporal understanding** — Describe what happens over TIME, not just what exists.
 * **Physics awareness** — 2026 models simulate cause-and-effect. Describe motion consequences, not just motion.
 * **Camera-as-character** — Camera movement is as important as subject movement.

* * *
## 📐 The Universal Prompt Scaffold (2026 Standard)
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#-the-universal-prompt-scaffold-2026-standard)
Professional prompts follow an **8-slot structure**. Every slot matters.

```
[SUBJECT] + [ACTION] + [ENVIRONMENT] + [CAMERA] + [LIGHTING] + [STYLE] + [MOTION] + [NEGATIVES]

```
 
| Slot | What it controls | Example | 
| --- | --- | --- | 
| **Subject** | Who/what is the focus | "A 30-year-old woman in a white linen blazer" | 
| **Action** | What is happening | "walking confidently through a glass-walled office" | 
| **Environment** | Where + atmosphere | "golden hour, city skyline through floor-to-ceiling windows" | 
| **Camera** | Shot type + movement | "35mm lens, slow dolly-in from medium to close-up" | 
| **Lighting** | Source, quality, mood | "warm 3200K key light, cool blue rim from window, Rembrandt lighting" | 
| **Style** | Aesthetic + color grade | "cinematic, Kodak 2383 film stock, teal-and-orange grade" | 
| **Motion** | Speed + physics | "slow-motion 60fps, hair slightly moving, suit fabric texture visible" | 
| **Negatives** | What to avoid | "no morphing, no extra limbs, no flickering, no cartoonish colors" | 
* * *
## ✅ The Golden Rules
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#-the-golden-rules)
### Rule 1 — Front-load the most important thing
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#rule-1--front-load-the-most-important-thing)

```
❌ "A sunset over the ocean, very beautiful, cinematic, a man is standing on a cliff"
✅ "A man standing on a cliff at sunset, overlooking a vast ocean, cinematic wide shot"

```

The AI processes the beginning first. Put the subject and action first, not the setting.
* * *
### Rule 2 — Specify length and physics of movement
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#rule-2--specify-length-and-physics-of-movement)

```
❌ "The camera moves"
✅ "Slow dolly-in from wide shot to close-up over 5 seconds"

❌ "Water splashes"
✅ "Water droplets scatter in slow motion, catching golden backlight, individual droplets visible"

```

* * *
### Rule 3 — Use cinematic language, not English descriptions
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#rule-3--use-cinematic-language-not-english-descriptions)
The models are trained on film datasets. Use film/photography terminology: 
| Natural language | Cinematic equivalent | 
| --- | --- | 
| "zooming in" | "dolly-in" or "push-in" | 
| "moving sideways" | "lateral tracking shot" | 
| "from above" | "bird's-eye view" / "overhead crane shot" | 
| "close to the face" | "extreme close-up (ECU)" | 
| "dark and moody" | "low-key lighting, noir aesthetic" | 
| "bright and clean" | "high-key lighting, minimal shadows" | 
| "blurry background" | "shallow depth of field, f/1.8 bokeh" | 
* * *
### Rule 4 — Prompt length sweet spot: 50–150 words
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#rule-4--prompt-length-sweet-spot-50150-words)
 * **Too short** (<30 words) → AI fills gaps with random choices
 * **Too long** (>200 words) → Contradictions confuse the model, outputs degrade
 * **Just right** (50–150 words) → Enough specificity without overloading

* * *
### Rule 5 — Always include negative constraints
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#rule-5--always-include-negative-constraints)
Every pro prompt ends with what NOT to do:

```
"...no morphing faces, no extra fingers, no flickering, no unnatural motion blur, 
no cartoonish saturation, no text overlays"

```

## 🎥 Camera Movement Vocabulary (Copy-Paste Ready)
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#-camera-movement-vocabulary-copy-paste-ready)
### Shots
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#shots) 
| Term | Effect | 
| --- | --- | 
| `extreme wide shot (EWS)` | Establishes location, subject small in frame | 
| `wide shot (WS)` | Full body, environment visible | 
| `medium shot (MS)` | Waist up, conversational feel | 
| `close-up (CU)` | Face/detail, emotional intimacy | 
| `extreme close-up (ECU)` | Single detail (eye, hand, product) | 
| `over-the-shoulder (OTS)` | POV feel, positions viewer | 
| `bird's-eye view` | Overhead, dramatic scale | 
| `low-angle shot` | Subject appears powerful/imposing | 
| `Dutch angle` | Tilted frame, tension/unease | 
### Movements
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#movements) 
| Term | Effect | 
| --- | --- | 
| `static shot` | No movement, stable and formal | 
| `dolly-in / push-in` | Slowly moves toward subject, builds intensity | 
| `dolly-out / pull-back` | Reveals context, creates wonder | 
| `pan left/right` | Horizontal rotation, follows action | 
| `tilt up/down` | Vertical rotation, reveals height | 
| `tracking shot` | Follows moving subject from side | 
| `crane shot` | Rises or descends dramatically | 
| `handheld / steadicam` | Slight shake = documentary realism | 
| `orbital / arc shot` | Circles around subject 360° | 
| `whip pan` | Fast horizontal blur, transitions |

## 🛠️ Platform-Specific Strategies
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#%EF%B8%8F-platform-specific-strategies)
### Sora 2 (OpenAI) — "Camera-First" Language
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#sora-2-openai--camera-first-language)
Sora 2 prioritizes temporal coherence. Describe **what changes over time** first.

```
Formula: [Camera move] + [Subject state change] + [Environment reaction]

✅ "Slow push-in from medium to extreme close-up as the woman's expression shifts 
from neutral to a subtle smile, city lights gradually blurring into bokeh behind her, 
warm amber tones, cinematic 2.39:1 aspect ratio"

```

**Sora-specific tips:**
 * Use "storyboard" input for multi-shot sequences
 * Specify physics explicitly: "the ball's trajectory follows realistic arc under gravity"
 * It handles complex action best — use it for physics-heavy scenes

* * *
### Runway Gen-4.5 — Keyframe + Motion Brush Workflow
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#runway-gen-45--keyframe--motion-brush-workflow)
Runway is best when combined with its visual tools, not just text.

```
Formula: [Reference image] + [Motion direction] + [Scene extension]

✅ "Extend this scene forward: the camera continues dollying right, 
revealing a wider room with floor-to-ceiling bookshelves, 
warm fireplace light warming the right side, 
wood grain texture visible, dust motes floating in light shafts"

```

**Runway-specific tips:**
 * Use **Motion Brush** to paint movement directions onto still images
 * Use **Act-Two** for performance capture from live video
 * Break long sequences into 5-second stitchable beats
 * Works best with short, precise beats: `"hand reaches forward, fingers wrap around glass, lifts slowly"`

* * *
### Kling 2.6 (Kuaishou) — Short Beats + Lip-Sync
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#kling-26-kuaishou--short-beats--lip-sync)
Kling is optimized for social-first content and long-duration video.

```
Formula: [Character description] + [Precise micro-action] + [Setting] + [Audio cue]

✅ "Young woman with expressive eyes and shoulder-length dark hair, 
laughing naturally while looking slightly off-camera, 
outdoor cafe in soft afternoon light, 
bokeh of green leaves behind her, 
natural breeze, casual and authentic mood"

```

**Kling-specific tips:**
 * Describe character features in detail — Kling maintains face consistency best
 * Excellent for lip-sync — provide audio script as separate input
 * For 1-2 minute videos, use **prompt chaining** (see below)
 * Specify `"subtle micro-expressions"` for realistic human emotion

* * *
### Google Veo 3.1 — Audio-Visual Sync Language
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#google-veo-31--audio-visual-sync-language)
Veo 3.1 natively generates audio, so describe the soundscape too.

```
Formula: [Visual scene] + [Audio environment] + [Camera] + [Style]

✅ "A barista crafting a latte in a morning cafe, 
steam hissing from the espresso machine, 
coffee pouring sound, soft jazz in background, 
medium close-up tilting down to the cup, 
warm yellow light, documentary style, 4K"

```

**Veo-specific tips:**
 * Describe ambient sounds, music style, and dialogue explicitly
 * Use `"sync audio to visual"` to ensure matched timing
 * Great for: cooking videos, nature, interviews, product demos

## 🔗 Advanced Technique: Prompt Chaining
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#-advanced-technique-prompt-chaining)
For longer videos, break them into **stitchable beats** — short sequential prompts that each extend the previous scene.
### Example — 30-second product ad broken into 6 beats:
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#example--30-second-product-ad-broken-into-6-beats)

```
Beat 1 (0-5s): 
"Extreme close-up of hands opening a matte black box, 
slow motion, soft studio light, premium unboxing aesthetic"

Beat 2 (5-10s):
"Continue: product (wireless headphones) lifted from box, 
rotating slowly to reveal design, 
shallow depth of field, focus pulls to product"

Beat 3 (10-15s):
"Cut to: person placing headphones over ears, eyes closed, 
slight smile, sound waves visualized in soft glow around headphones"

Beat 4 (15-20s):
"Cut to: urban street, person walking confidently, 
headphones visible, tracking shot from side, 
bokeh city lights, golden hour"

Beat 5 (20-25s):
"Aerial pull-back showing person small in frame, 
city scale revealed, music visualizer fades in overlay"

Beat 6 (25-30s):
"Product hero shot: headphones on minimal white surface, 
360 orbit, logo appears, fade to white"

```

## ❌ Common Mistakes to Avoid
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#-common-mistakes-to-avoid) 
| Mistake | Problem | Fix | 
| --- | --- | --- | 
| "Make a cool video" | Too vague — random output | Specify subject + action + style | 
| "Beautiful cinematic amazing stunning" | Adjective stacking wastes tokens | 1-2 strong descriptors max | 
| Contradicting instructions | "Dark and moody but bright and colorful" | Pick one mood | 
| No camera direction | AI defaults to static shot | Always specify camera movement | 
| Forgetting negatives | Gets extra fingers, morphing faces | Always add negative constraints | 
| 200+ word prompts | Contradictions confuse the model | Keep 50–150 words | 
| Starting with setting | Setting gets priority over subject | Start with subject + action | 
| Generic time-of-day | "At night" = any darkness | Specify: "blue hour", "neon-lit", "moonlit" |

## 📊 Prompt Quality Checklist
[](https://gist.github.com/syscraft906/44566294675ccf4e81c5f40a7f3de313#-prompt-quality-checklist)
Before generating, verify:
 * Subject described specifically (not "a person" but "a 28-year-old man in grey suit")
 * Action described with motion verb (not "walking" but "walks purposefully toward camera")
 * Camera shot type specified (wide / medium / close-up / ECU)
 * Camera movement specified (static / dolly / tracking / handheld)
 * Lighting described (time of day OR lighting style)
 * Style/aesthetic keyword included (cinematic / commercial / documentary)
 * Negative constraints included
 * Prompt is 50–150 words
 * Most important elements are in first 30 words
