#  Insurance & Payment Options
##  Understanding Your Dental Insurance at Butterfly Dental
At Butterfly Dental, we believe in providing transparent information about dental insurance to help you make informed decisions about your dental care. We accept all PPO (Preferred Provider Organization) dental insurance plans, and we're committed to helping you maximize your insurance benefits while receiving the highest quality dental care-- however, we are out of network with all providers. Read on to understand how this actually benefits you!
##  Insurance Network Status
Butterfly Dental is an out-of-network provider with dental insurance companies. However, this doesn't mean you cannot be treated at our office or use your insurance benefits. In fact, many of our patients choose us specifically because we're out-of-network, and here's why:
###  Financial Benefits
When we're not bound by insurance company contracts, we have more flexibility to offer you better value:
We can provide additional discounts on many dental treatments that in-network offices cannot offer due to contract restrictions
We work directly with you to maximize your insurance benefits
You maintain control over your dental care decisions rather than having them dictated by insurance company policies

###  Quality of Care Advantages
Being out-of-network allows us to prioritize your care without compromise:
We can use the highest quality materials and latest dental technologies without insurance company restrictions
We're free to recommend the best treatment options for your specific needs, not just what insurance will cover
We can offer advanced procedures and materials that many in-network providers cannot, including laser-assisted treatments and premium crown materials
##  Understanding In-Network vs. Out-of-Network
**In-Network Providers:**
Have signed contracts with insurance companies
Must follow strict fee schedules and treatment guidelines
May have limitations on materials and procedures they can use
Often have restrictions on offering additional discounts
**Out-of-Network Providers (like Butterfly Dental):**
Work directly with patients while still accepting insurance
Can offer flexible pricing and additional discounts
Have the freedom to use premium materials and advanced technologies
Can focus on providing the best care without insurance company restrictions
##  Flexible Payment Options
We understand that dental care is an investment in your health. To make quality care accessible to all our patients, we offer several payment solutions:
**Flexible Payment Options:**
We accept CareCredit, a healthcare credit card that allows you to finance your dental treatments with convenient monthly payments. This option can help make your dental care more manageable and affordable.
![Care-Credit-Logo \(1\)](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20550%2079'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
**Out-of-Network Providers (like Butterfly Dental):**
We provide in-office financing with zero down payment (restrictions may apply for certain treatments). This flexible payment option allows you to proceed with necessary dental work while managing your budget effectively.
##  Our Commitment to You
We understand that dental care is an investment in your health. Our team is dedicated to helping you:
Understand your insurance benefits
Maximize your coverage
Create treatment plans that balance your dental needs with your financial considerations
Find flexible payment solutions when needed
Many of our patients find that the superior care, materials, and service they receive at Butterfly Dental make it worth choosing us as their out-of-network provider. We're happy to discuss your specific insurance plan and explain how we can work together to help you achieve optimal dental health while managing costs effectively.
Contact our office today to learn more about how we can work with your specific insurance plan or to schedule a consultation

##  Affordable Dental Care
At Butterfly Dental, we believe quality dental care should be accessible to everyone. While we operate as an out-of-network provider, our unique approach actually helps make dental care more affordable for our patients. By eliminating the overhead costs and administrative burdens associated with insurance network contracts, we're able to pass these savings directly to you through flexible pricing and special discounts that in-network providers cannot offer.
We provide transparent pricing, accept all PPO insurance plans, and work diligently to maximize your insurance benefits. Our team will help you understand your coverage, explore payment options, and create a treatment plan that fits your budget without compromising on the quality of care. Whether you need preventive care, restorative treatments, or cosmetic procedures, we're committed to making your dental care both exceptional and affordable.
