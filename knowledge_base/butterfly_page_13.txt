##  Fotona Laser Dentistry 
The Fotona Laser is a revolutionary device that can penetrate both hard and soft tissue in the mouth. This laser replaces the traditional dental drill, which eliminates the sound, pain and vibrations that often cause patients to fear the dental office.
##  TwinLight® Gum Treatment 
TwinLight® Periodontal Treatment (TPT) is an advanced laser-assisted therapy used to treat periodontal disease. It is minimally invasive and leverages two laser wavelengths: Er and Nd, which work together to enhance tissue healing and disinfection, offering an alternative to traditional surgical methods.
##  Laser Root Canal 
When tooth pulp becomes infected or inflamed due to deep decay, repeated dental procedures, or injury, a root canal may be necessary to save the tooth.
