##  Nightlase®
By tightening the soft tissues in the mouth area, NightLase® reduces the vibration and collapse of the airway that causes snoring. It also improves airflow, reducing symptoms of sleep apnea.

##  ComfortLase™
ComfortLase™ is an advanced laser technology used in dentistry and medical primarily for pain management, soft tissue treatments, and promoting wound healing. It helps reduce inflammation, pain, and accelerates tissue regeneration, making it an effective option for healing after surgery, chemotherapy, TMJ, trauma.
##  TightWave®/IntimaWave®
TightWave®/IntimaWave® is a non-invasive treatment that uses High-Intensity Tesla Magnetic Stimulation (HITS™) technology, to strengthen and tone muscles.

##  TonsileLase®
TonsileLase® is a laser treatment that addresses conditions related to the tonsils, such as chronic inflammation or tonsillar hypertrophy, often used as an alternative to traditional tonsillectomy.
##  NaseLase®
When nostrils or nasal valve collapse due to trauma, NaseLase® can improve nasal airflow, making it easier for patients to breathe through their nose and potentially reduce snoring or other sleep-related breathing issues​.

##  Silent Ear
Silent Ear utilizes low-dose laser therapy to target tinnitus symptoms. This advanced treatment employs specific wavelengths of light to stimulate cellular repair and reduce inflammation in the inner ear. The procedure is non-invasive and painless, typically delivered in multiple sessions. Clinical studies suggest improvements in tinnitus-related discomfort, sound perception, and quality of life for many patients.
##  Sleep Apnea Treatment
Dentists play an important role in diagnosing and managing sleep apnea, particularly obstructive sleep apnea (OSA), which occurs when the airway becomes blocked during sleep, causing pauses in breathing. Treatment options including Oral Appliance Therapy (OAT); Mandibular advancement devices (MADs); Nightlase® treatment.

##  TMJ Comforting Treatment
TMJ (Temporomandibular Joint) treatment combines multiple therapeutic approaches to address jaw pain and dysfunction. Treatment options include laser therapy for pain reduction and inflammation, bite adjustment, custom oral appliances, and jaw exercises. This comprehensive approach helps alleviate symptoms such as jaw clicking, limited mouth opening, facial pain, and headaches while promoting proper joint function and healing.
##  Post Covid Symptoms
Oral health issues might be direct effects of the virus, immune response, or secondary complications resulting from the infection. Common dental-related post-COVID symptoms include: Dry Mouth (Xerostomia); Altered Taste (Dysgeusia):Mouth Ulcers or Sores; Oral Thrush (Candidiasis); Jaw Pain and TMJ Disorders; With PBM ( photobiomodulation ) laser energy, most of those symptoms can get great improvements.
